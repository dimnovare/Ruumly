# Ruumly "Kolimise Kontrolltorn" Reel Design

Date: 2026-08-04

## Objective

Create one polished Estonian vertical social ad for Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn. The ad must explain Ruumly's current direction clearly:

> Moving is a project. Ruumly puts it together.

Ruumly connects storage, movers, trailers, vans, packing, cleaning, and insurance through one request. Users can get 2-3 offers from verified partners or browse the directory themselves. Partners list for free; paid boosts and special placement are optional.

## Audience

Estonian consumers who are moving, renovating, clearing space, or arranging temporary storage. The ad should feel local, useful, and a little funny without becoming silly or cheap.

## Single Message

**Kolimine ei ole üks asi. See on väike projekt.**

The viewer should understand within the first seconds that a move often becomes many small tasks. Ruumly reduces that into one request.

## Creative Concept

Concept A: **Kolimise kontrolltorn**.

At the start, a person opens a moving box expecting one simple task. Instead, labels/tasks spill out: `ladu`, `kolija`, `haagis`, `kaubik`, `pakkimine`, `koristus`, `kindlustus`. The shock moment lands around second 3. This is the hook: funny recognition, not slapstick.

Ruumly then becomes the calm control panel for the move. The ad shifts from moving chaos to one clean request flow and a simple offer result.

## Hook

The hook must land at approximately 3 seconds.

Timeline:

- `0.0-1.4`: Calm setup. A person sits among moving boxes and opens one box.
- `1.4-2.8`: The box reveals a ridiculous stack of tasks, forms, notes, and mini service labels.
- `2.8-3.4`: Shock line lands visually and in voice: "Kolimine ei ole üks asi."
- `3.4-5.0`: Resolve: "See on väike projekt."

The hook should feel like a funny truth: one move secretly contains seven jobs.

## Final Voiceover

Language: Estonian.

Target duration: 22-25 seconds. The edit must leave silence after the last sentence so no phrase is clipped.

Script:

```text
Kolimine ei ole üks asi.
See on väike projekt.

Ladu, kolija, haagis, kaubik,
pakkimine, koristus ja kindlustus.

Ruumlys kirjeldad kõik ühe päringuga.
Meie aitame leida sobivad kinnitatud partnerid
ja saad 2-3 pakkumist rahulikuks võrdlemiseks.

Ruumly.
Kümne kõne asemel üks päring.
```

Voice direction:

- Warm Estonian adult speaker.
- Clear, modern, trustworthy.
- Slight smile in the hook.
- Calm confidence after Ruumly appears.
- No radio-ad shouting.
- No rushed delivery.
- Each sentence must end before the next visual beat cuts.

Audio rule: final export must be checked against waveform and subtitles/titles. No edit may cut mid-word or mid-sentence.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 - The Box Has Too Many Tasks

Time: `0.0-5.0`.

Visual: Northern European apartment, moving boxes, daylight. Person opens one box. Instead of one simple item, task cards or abstract papers pop out around them.

On-screen text:

- `Kolimine?`
- `See ei ole üks asi.`

No readable third-party company names. No competitor logos.

### Scene 2 - The Move Becomes a Project

Time: `5.0-10.5`.

Visual: Elegant montage, slower than previous versions. Show 4-5 clear service moments, not all seven as a frantic sprint:

- storage space
- movers with boxes
- trailer or van
- packing/cleaning
- protected belongings/insurance cue

On-screen text chips:

- `Ladu`
- `Kolija`
- `Haagis / kaubik`
- `Pakkimine ja koristus`
- `Kindlustus`

### Scene 3 - Ruumly Organizes It

Time: `10.5-17.0`.

Visual: Real Ruumly UI recording. Show selecting multiple needs and moving through the request form. The UI must be real production or a safe local recording of the same real interface.

On-screen text:

- `Kirjelda kõik ühe päringuga.`

No personal data. Demo inputs only.

### Scene 4 - Offers Without the Chase

Time: `17.0-21.5`.

Visual: Designed offer result, or real UI if available and clean. Show 2-3 options arriving in one place. Do not imply instant automated matching if the concierge loop is involved.

On-screen text:

- `2-3 pakkumist`
- `Kinnitatud partneritelt`

### Scene 5 - Brand CTA

Time: `21.5-25.0`.

Visual: Ruumly logo on clean navy/teal brand background.

Final text:

- `Kümne kõne asemel üks päring.`
- `ruumly.eu`

CTA:

- `Kirjelda, mida vajad`

## Visual Style

- Premium realistic Baltic/Nordic commercial.
- Natural daylight, practical interiors, calm colors.
- Ruumly navy and teal accents.
- Clean typography with large mobile-safe text.
- Text overlays must not cover faces, the Ruumly UI, or important service details.
- Use humor in the situation, not in goofy acting.

Avoid:

- Generated readable fake websites.
- Competitor logos.
- White flashes.
- Glitch transitions.
- Overcrowded service montage.
- Tiny subtitles.
- Reusing the rejected V3 hook.
