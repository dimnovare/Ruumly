# Ruumly One Request Reel Recut Design

## Goal

Rebuild the Ruumly social reel so the ad clearly explains the new customer-facing promise: one request can cover storage, moving, trailer rental, and related move services. The revised cut must feel playful and professional, avoid duplicated UI screens, and keep the voice-over understandable.

## Problems To Fix

- The 15-second cut has unclear voice-over around second 10 and uses a voice that feels less suitable than the main cut opening.
- The 23.5-second and 30-second cuts have unclear, clipped voice-over around second 5.
- The service-selection screen appears more than once, making the video feel repetitive.
- The 30-second ending holds on an empty contact form for too long.
- The hook is not funny or sharp enough for a social ad.

## Creative Direction

Use a single stronger concept for all exports: **One Request, Many Answers**.

The video starts with a moving person overwhelmed by too many browser tabs, calls, boxes, and service decisions. At the three-second mark, the hook lands with a quick refusal of the chaos. Ruumly then becomes the simple alternative: describe the need once, see all relevant services at one glance, and receive provider options.

The ad should sell the workflow, not the catalogue. The key emotional shift is from messy comparison to calm choice.

## Final Timeline

### 23-25 Second Master

- 0.0-3.2s: Hook. Moving chaos escalates. At second 3, the voice-over lands on a short, clear punch.
- 3.2-6.2s: Ruumly transition. One request replaces scattered searching.
- 6.2-10.5s: Real Ruumly request UI, showing service choices once.
- 10.5-15.5s: Seven services appear quickly as distinct visuals: storage, moving, trailer, cleaning, packing, van rental, insurance.
- 15.5-20.5s: Offer/result payoff. Show a designed email or option-card moment with provider offers arriving.
- 20.5-24.0s: Brand CTA: Ruumly, one request, several options, ruumly.eu.

### 15 Second Cutdown

- Keep the same story, compressed.
- Use the same voice family as the master.
- Do not squeeze the full service list into dense speech; show services visually.
- End with the offer/result payoff, not an empty form.

### 30 Second Cutdown

- Use the extra time for the result flow: request submitted, options arrive, choose one.
- Avoid static holds longer than 1.5 seconds unless copy is changing or cards are animating.
- Do not repeat the service-selection screen.

## Voice-Over

Regenerate all voice-over with the same voice and style settings as the preferred opening of the previous 23.5-second cut. Use shorter lines, slower pacing, and cleaner pauses.

Primary Estonian script:

```text
Kolimine tulemas?
Ära ava seitset eri lehte.

Kirjelda Ruumlys üks kord, mida vajad:
ladu, kolimine, haagis või muu abi.

Meie aitame leida sobivad pakkumised.
Sina valid rahulikult.

Ruumly. Üks päring, mitu võimalust.
```

## Visual Rules

- No burned subtitles.
- Designed copy may appear as brand/UI labels only.
- Do not reuse old campaign footage as final source.
- One service-selection view maximum.
- The ending must show progress or payoff: provider options, email arrival, or a clear CTA.
- Keep mobile-safe margins for Instagram and Facebook Reels.

- Revised 30s cutdown MP4.
- Clean versions without voice when practical.
