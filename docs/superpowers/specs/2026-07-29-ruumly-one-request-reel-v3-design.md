# Ruumly One Request Reel V3 Design

## Goal

Produce a clearer V3 of the Ruumly moving/request reel. The V2 clips are visually usable, but the pacing is too fast, key voice-over lines cross visual cuts, overlays cover useful picture areas, and the hook is not memorable enough.

## Corrections

- Hook must show a simple, readable gag: a person hidden behind a large moving box pops out at around second 3.
- The first spoken promise must include `Üks päring`.
- The line `Ruumly aitab leida sobivad pakkumised` must be fully audible and must not overlap a major visual chapter change.
- Service visuals must stay on screen long enough to understand what is offered.
- Overlay text must move out of the useful image area. Use only small corner chips during footage.
- Result screen copy must be clearer than `3 pakkumist saabus`.
- `Võrdle ja vali rahulikult` must live inside the designed result/CTA area, not over a busy frame.

## V3 Voice

Main:

```text
Kolimine tulemas?

Üks päring.

Ruumly aitab leida sobivad pakkumised.

Ladu, kolimine, haagis või muu abi.

Kirjelda, mida vajad.
Pakkumised tulevad ühte kohta.

Sina võrdled ja valid rahulikult.

Ruumly.
```

15s:

```text
Kolimine tulemas?

Üks päring.

Ruumly aitab leida sobivad pakkumised:
ladu, kolimine, haagis või muu abi.

Sina valid rahulikult.

Ruumly.
```

30s:

```text
Kolimine tulemas?

Üks päring.

Ruumly aitab leida sobivad pakkumised.

Vali korraga: ladu, kolimine, haagis või muu abi.

Lisa linn, aeg ja väike täpsustus.

Pakkumised tulevad ühte kohta.

Sina võrdled ja valid rahulikult.

Ruumly.
```

## Timeline Principles

- 15s cut uses fewer visual beats. It must not try to show every service as a separate full scene.
- 24s master gives the service section about 8 seconds.
- 30s gives service and result scenes enough room to breathe.
- No major visual transition may occur mid-sentence.
- No burned subtitles.

## Result Screen

Replace `3 pakkumist saabus` with `Pakkumised ühes kohas`.

Use cards:

- `Laopind`
- `Kolimisabi`
- `Haagis või kaubik`

Use the final decision line:

- `Võrdle ja vali rahulikult`
